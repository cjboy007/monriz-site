Monriz Ice Cream Cones are jam-filled marshmallow cones: a 3 g piece, packed 30 to a 300 g jar. The format is built for retail shelves and gift packs, and the jar size and piece weight are the confirmed specification. They are supplied to importers and private-label brands, with one 20ft container as the minimum order.

Typical applications: Retail jars · Gift packs · Dessert decoration

Buyer FAQ

What is the pack format for ice cream cone marshmallows?
A 3 g cone, 30 pieces to a 300 g jar. Other jar sizes are confirmed per SKU in the quotation.
What is the minimum order?
One 20ft container, and mixed SKUs are possible. The per-SKU minimum is confirmed in the quotation.
Are Monriz ice cream cones halal certified?
No. We do not currently hold a halal certificate. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and the ingredient declaration is available on request.
What is the lead time?
20 to 25 days after artwork approval, typical. The exact date is confirmed per order.
Can you send samples before an order?
Yes. Sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per SKU before dispatch.
