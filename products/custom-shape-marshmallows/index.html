Buyer FAQ

Can you produce a shape that is not on your list?
Yes, subject to a mould. Send the drawing or reference and we confirm mould cost, sampling and minimum quantity before the order.
What is the minimum order for custom shapes?
One 20ft container for standard shapes. A new mould may carry its own minimum, confirmed in the quotation.
Are Monriz custom shapes halal certified?
No. We do not currently hold a halal certificate. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and the ingredient declaration is available on request.
What is the lead time?
20 to 25 days after artwork approval for existing moulds, typical. A new mould adds sampling time, confirmed per project.
Can you send samples before an order?
Yes. Sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per shape before dispatch.
