MonrizMonriz — Marshmallow Factory in Anbu, Chaozhou

Monriz is the export brand of Chaozhou Chao'an Anbu Yuxiangyuan Food Co., Ltd., a marshmallow manufacturer in Anbu, Chaozhou, Guangdong, China. The plant runs extruded, deposited and jam-filled lines and packs for retail and bulk buyers. Food production licence SC11344512103958 is held under the Anbu manufacturing registration, 潮州市潮安区庵埠裕香园食品有限公司.

Company

Legal name: 潮州市潮安区庵埠裕香园食品有限公司 (Chaozhou Chao'an Anbu Yuxiangyuan Food Co., Ltd.). Brand: Monriz. Location: Liulong Industrial Zone, Anbu, Chao'an, Chaozhou, Guangdong, China. The plant holds food production licence SC11344512103958.

Factory

The plant runs aerated-candy lines for extruded, deposited and jam-filled marshmallows. Packaging covers pillow bags, stand-up pouches, tubs, jars and bulk cartons. Quality control includes batch testing, metal detection and lot traceability on every order.

Products

Six product lines: classic cylinders, twisted ropes, custom shapes, ice cream cones, jam-filled and mini marshmallows. Every line is available under the Monriz brand or as private label.

Export

Containers ship from Shantou and Shenzhen on FOB, CIF or CFR terms. Export documents include commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and health certificate. Sea transit times shown on the home page are approximate.

Quality

We hold a Chinese food production licence. Halal, HACCP and ISO 22000 certificates are not held. Ingredient declarations, batch test reports and third-party inspection can be arranged before an order.
