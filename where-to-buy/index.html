Where to Buy Monriz MarshmallowsWhere to Buy Monriz Marshmallows

Monriz products reach buyers through local distributors and retail partners in Singapore, Malaysia and South Africa. Retail listings are managed by independent sellers and vary by store and season. For samples, current availability and private-label orders, contact the export team directly.

Distributor markets

Public retail listings for Monriz have appeared in Singapore and Malaysia through Shopee sellers, and in South Africa through Giant promotional catalogues. These listings are run by local distributors or retailers, not by Monriz. We do not control their stock, pricing or delivery terms.

Request samples first

We encourage buyers to request samples directly from the factory before placing a retail order. Samples let you check texture, piece size, packaging and label copy; sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per SKU.

Private label and wholesale

For private-label or container orders, contact sales@monrizcandy.com with the product line, volume and destination port. The minimum order is one 20ft container, mixed SKUs are possible, and typical lead time is 20 to 25 days after artwork approval.

Buyer FAQ

Can I buy Monriz marshmallows directly from this website?
We are the export team, not a retail store. We supply importers, distributors and private-label buyers by the container. For retail purchases, use the local listings; for samples and wholesale, contact us.
Which markets have Monriz retail listings?
Public listings have appeared in Singapore, Malaysia and South Africa. Availability varies by seller and season; ask us for the current status before you rely on a listing.
Are the Shopee or Giant listings official Monriz stores?
No. They are independent distributor or retailer listings. Monriz does not operate those stores and does not control their stock or pricing.
How do I request a sample?
Email sales@monrizcandy.com with the product line, destination country and courier account if you have one. Sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per SKU.

Ask for the current document set.

Tell us the product line, volume and destination port. We reply with packing details, MOQ and the documents we can provide.
