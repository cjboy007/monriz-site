Private Label MarshmallowsPrivate Label Marshmallows — OEM Factory for Importers

Monriz runs private label and OEM programmes for importers, distributors and retail brands. Colour, flavour, weight, packing format and artwork are agreed before sampling. The minimum order is one 20ft container, and lead time is 20 to 25 days after artwork approval, typical.

What can be customised

  • Colour and flavour
  • Shape, including a new mould
  • Piece weight and bag size
  • Packing format: bag, tub, jar or bulk carton
  • Your artwork and brand on the pack
  • Arabic and English labels

Artwork and labels

Send artwork as AI or PDF. We prepare a pre-press proof and confirm label content before production. Arabic and English labels are both supported, including ingredients, production date and expiry date. Label rules for the destination market stay with the buyer, and we flag anything the proof cannot meet.

Sampling

Samples are available before an order. Sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per SKU. A new mould adds sampling time, confirmed per project.

MOQ and lead time

One 20ft container, and mixed SKUs are possible. The per-SKU minimum is confirmed in the quotation. Production takes 20 to 25 days after artwork approval, typical, and the exact date is confirmed per order.

Certification

Halal and HACCP certificates are not held. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and the ingredient declaration is available on request. If your private label requires a certificate we do not hold, tell us before sampling.

Buyer FAQ

Do you accept small private label orders?
The minimum is one 20ft container, and mixed SKUs are possible. Per-SKU minimums are confirmed in the quotation.
Can you print our brand and artwork?
Yes. Send artwork as AI or PDF. We prepare a pre-press proof and confirm bag size, material and label copy before production.
Can you produce our own shape?
Yes, subject to a mould. Send the drawing or reference and we confirm mould cost, sampling and minimum quantity before the order.
Do you provide Arabic labels?
Yes. Arabic and English labels are both available, including ingredients, production date and expiry date.
How long does sampling take?
Sample lead time is confirmed per SKU. A new mould adds sampling time, confirmed per project.
Is halal certification available for private label?
No. We do not currently hold a halal certificate. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and the ingredient declaration is available for your certifier.
