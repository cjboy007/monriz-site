China Marshmallow Export Guide

Three things decide whether a marshmallow order from China lands on time: whether the plant may export, whether the document set is accepted at your port, and whether the shipping plan matches the production calendar. Price comes after those three.

Written for first and second time importers, using the Chaozhou candy cluster as the example.

The short version

Ask for two numbers before you discuss price: the food production licence and the export food producer filing number. A plant can hold a licence and still not be filed for export. Then map the documents your broker needs, and only then set a shipment date.

First orders slip for four reasons: artwork revisions, a public holiday shutdown, a document that was never issued, or a container booked after production instead of before it.

Who may export food from China

RequirementWhat it isHow a buyer checks it
Food production licenceIssued under Chinese food safety law, printed as an SC number on the pack. Names the product category and the plant address.Query the number on the market regulation platform, and the company on the national credit system.
Export food producer filingRequired by the Food Safety Law and Customs Decree 249. The plant files with its local customs office and receives a filing number.Ask for the filing certificate or number. It is free, and the committed processing time is three working days.
Destination registrationSome markets require the foreign plant to be registered before import, separate from the Chinese filing.Ask your broker whether your market requires plant registration for confectionery.

The filing is the step that ties a plant to export inspection. A factory selling only at home may never have filed, and a trading company cannot file on the plant's behalf.

Two traps. Health certificate wording is market specific, so a certificate that satisfied one importer may be rejected by another. And a certificate of origin proves origin, not quality.

Ports and routings

The Chaozhou candy cluster sits about an hour by road from Shantou, and most regional candy exports move through the Guangao terminal. Guangao added direct services to major Southeast Asian ports at the end of 2023, and runs a service toward the Middle East that calls Dammam.

Lead time, broken into its parts

Production lead time is not the number you plan a launch on.

  1. Sample request to sample in hand, confirmed per SKU. A new mould or film adds a round.
  2. Artwork proof and approval, usually the longest delay, looping between your designer, the printer and the Arabic proofreader.
  3. Film or plate preparation. New designs need new print cylinders before the run starts.
  4. Production, typically 20 to 25 days after artwork approval, confirmed per order.
  5. Booking and container release, done while production runs.
  6. Inland haulage, customs declaration and loading.
  7. Sailing and transit.

Plan backwards from the date the goods must be on shelf. Three calendar factors catch first time buyers: Chinese New Year, when most factories stop for two to four weeks; the National Day holiday in the first week of October; and the South China typhoon season from July to September, which can close a terminal for a day or two.

Terms, payment and money at risk

FOB, CIF and CFR are all normal. FOB leaves booking to you and keeps the freight transparent. CIF is easier for a first order but hides the freight margin, so ask for the freight rate separately even when you buy CIF.

Payment is usually T/T with a deposit and the balance against the bill of lading copy, or a letter of credit at sight. Under a credit, every document must match exactly, including spelling and weights. Discrepancies are the most common cause of payment delay, and usually come from a description copied out of a quotation.

Watch free time at destination. Demurrage and detention clock starts at discharge and at gate out, and Gulf terminals are strict. If your broker needs a week, book clearance before the vessel arrives.

FAQ

Which port do Chinese marshmallow factories ship from?

Most of the Chaozhou cluster ships through Shantou's Guangao terminal, with Shenzhen as the alternative when a buyer needs more sailings or a specific carrier.

Do I need a health certificate for marshmallows?

It depends on your market. Gulf and many African authorities ask for one, and the accepted wording differs. Send the requirement to the supplier before production, because the certificate is issued per shipment after inspection.

Is a certificate of origin the same as a free trade certificate?

No. A general certificate states where goods were made. A preferential certificate reduces duty, and it exists only where a trade agreement covers your destination and the product.

How long does a first order take from deposit to arrival?

Sample round, artwork approval, 20 to 25 days of production after approval, then sea transit. First orders with new artwork routinely take two weeks longer than repeat orders.

What happens if the label is rejected at the port?

The importer carries the cost. The options are relabelling in a customs warehouse, re-export or destruction, and all three are expensive. Approve a printed sample, not a PDF.
