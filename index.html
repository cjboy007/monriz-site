About

The factory behind Monriz.

Monriz is the export brand of Chaozhou Chao'an Anbu Yuxiangyuan Food Co., Ltd., a marshmallow manufacturer in Anbu, Chaozhou, the candy town of southern China. Our plant runs aerated-candy lines for extruded, deposited and jam-filled marshmallows, and packs them for retail and bulk buyers across the Middle East and select export markets. Food production licence SC11344512103958 is held under the company's Anbu manufacturing registration, 潮州市潮安区庵埠裕香园食品有限公司.

Product fit

Which line fits which channel.

Three lines cover most orders: Classic Cylinders, Twisted Ropes and Custom Shapes. They differ in shape and in the channel they sell through, not in the MOQ of one 20ft container or in the lead time.

Classic Cylinders

100 g – 1 kg bags in white, pink or mixed colours. Built for retail shelves, bakery and dessert decoration, and for distributors who need a steady everyday line.

Twisted Ropes

Two- and four-colour twists with fruit flavours, in bags or tubs. Built for pick-and-mix counters, specialist retail and dessert decoration, where the shelf needs a different shape from the cylinder.

Custom Shapes

Hearts, flowers and Ramadan or Eid editions, produced to your mould or ours. Built for private-label ranges, seasonal promotions and gift packs; the mould and minimum are confirmed in the quotation.

Lines and formats

Monriz makes 6 marshmallow lines. Classic Cylinders come in 100 g – 1 kg bags, Twisted Ropes in two or four colours, Custom Shapes to your mould or ours, Ice Cream Cones as a 3 g piece packed 30 to a 300 g jar, Jam-Filled individually wrapped, and Mini Marshmallows in retail bags or bulk. Every line runs under your brand or ours as OEM.

MOQ, lead time and shipping

Lead time is 20–25 days after artwork approval, confirmed per order. Three points are fixed in the quotation: sample lead time, courier cost and the per-SKU minimum. The MOQ is one 20ft container with mixed SKUs. Goods ship from Shantou and Shenzhen on FOB, CIF or CFR terms, with commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin.

Certificates and verification

We do not hold 3 certificates: halal, HACCP and ISO 22000, and we say so before you set requirements. What we hold is the Chinese food production licence SC11344512103958, open to verification. Ingredient declarations and batch test reports are sent on request, and third-party inspection by SGS or Intertek can be arranged before an order.

Buyer FAQ

Questions importers ask before ordering.

These are the answers we give importers before they request a quotation: who makes the marshmallows, whether halal or HACCP certificates are held, the MOQ, the lead time, the ports we ship from and the documents we provide. Each answer is written in the same terms we use in the quotation, with no estimates.

Who manufactures Monriz marshmallows?
Monriz is the export brand of Chaozhou Chao'an Anbu Yuxiangyuan Food Co., Ltd., a marshmallow manufacturer in Anbu, Chaozhou, Guangdong, China. The plant runs extruded, deposited and jam-filled lines and packs for retail and bulk buyers.
Are Monriz marshmallows halal certified?
No. We do not currently hold a halal certificate. Our standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and we can send the ingredient declaration so your certifier can assess them. Tell us before you order if your market requires a certificate.
Do you hold HACCP or ISO 22000?
No. We hold a Chinese food production licence, SC11344512103958. Third-party inspection by SGS or Intertek can be arranged before an order.
What is the minimum order quantity?
One 20ft container, and mixed SKUs are possible. Per-SKU minimums are confirmed in the quotation.
Can you do private label or OEM?
Yes. Colour, flavour, weight, packaging and artwork can be customised. Arabic and English labels are both available.
What is the typical lead time?
20 to 25 days after artwork approval, typical and confirmed per order. Sample lead time is confirmed per SKU.
Which ports do you ship from?
Shantou and Shenzhen, on FOB, CIF or CFR terms. Sea transit times in the table are approximate.
Can you send samples before an order?
Yes. Sample lead time and courier cost are confirmed per SKU before dispatch.
What documents do you provide?
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and health certificate. Ingredient declarations and batch test reports are available on request. Halal and HACCP certificates are not available.
