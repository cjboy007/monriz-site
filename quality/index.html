Quality & DocumentationQuality & Documentation — What We Hold and What We Do Not

Monriz manufactures in Anbu, Chaozhou under Chinese food production licence SC11344512103958. We do not hold a halal certificate, a HACCP certificate or an ISO 22000 certificate, and we say so before a buyer specifies them. This page lists the documents we can provide and how an audit is handled.

Production licence

The food production licence number is SC11344512103958. It is held under the Anbu manufacturing registration, 潮州市潮安区庵埠裕香园食品有限公司, the plant of Chaozhou Chao'an Anbu Yuxiangyuan Food Co., Ltd. The licence certificate can be shared for verification before an order.

Certification status

Halal: not held. HACCP: not held. ISO 22000: not held. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours. Ingredient declarations are available so your certifier can assess the formulation. If your market requires a certificate we do not hold, tell us before you commit, and we will say whether we can support the order.

Documents we can provide

  • Business licence
  • Food production licence SC11344512103958
  • Ingredient declaration
  • Batch test report
  • Certificate of origin
  • Health certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list

Audits and inspection

Third-party inspection by SGS or Intertek can be arranged before an order. Video walkthroughs and on-site audits are welcome. We do not claim a certified quality management system, so the audit scope is agreed case by case.

Labelling

Labels can be produced in Arabic and English, with ingredients, production date and expiry date. Market-specific requirements are confirmed per order before artwork is approved.

Buyer FAQ

Do you have a halal certificate?
No. We do not currently hold a halal certificate. Standard recipes are pork-free and contain no alcohol-based flavours, and the ingredient declaration is available so your certifier can assess it.
Do you have HACCP or ISO 22000?
No. Neither certificate is held. We hold a Chinese food production licence, SC11344512103958, and third-party inspection can be arranged before an order.
What food safety documents can you send?
Business licence, food production licence, ingredient declaration, batch test report, certificate of origin and health certificate. The first two are available immediately, the rest are issued per order or per shipment.
Can we audit the factory before ordering?
Yes. Video walkthroughs and on-site audits are welcome, and third-party inspection by SGS or Intertek can be arranged. The scope is agreed before the visit.
Can you print Arabic labels?
Yes. Arabic and English labels are both available, including ingredients, production date and expiry date. Label content is confirmed against your market requirements before print.
What is the food production licence number?
SC11344512103958, held under the Anbu manufacturing registration 潮州市潮安区庵埠裕香园食品有限公司.
